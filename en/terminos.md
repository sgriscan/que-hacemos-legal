## 3. Your account

- You are responsible for keeping your password safe and for what is done with your account.
- The data you provide must be true.
- Your display name must follow the Community Rules: it can't be offensive or impersonate someone else.
- You can delete your account whenever you want from **Account → Delete my account**.

## 4. What the app doesn't guarantee

The information about plans (times, prices, venues, whether a plan actually happens) is entered by the app's team or by external organizers. We do our best to keep it accurate, but **we don't guarantee it is**, nor that the plan takes place as described.

The app **doesn't sell tickets.** Ticket links lead to third-party sites, which have their own terms. Before opening them, the app shows you which site you are going to.

## 5. The content you post

The comments you write, and if you are an organizer your plans, photos and organizer profile, **remain yours**. By posting them you give us a free, non-exclusive permission for two uses:

- **showing them in the app**, while they are published;
- **using them to promote the app**: on social media, on its website and in other promotional materials. We may show them in screenshots or adapt them to each medium's format (cropping them, for example), without changing their meaning.

**Your display name and profile photo don't appear in those promotional pieces**: we cover or crop them, unless you authorize us separately. An organizer's name, on the other hand, may appear, since it is part of the plan being promoted.

**If you delete your content, or your account,** we stop using it in new pieces. Pieces already published may remain visible where they were published; if you ask us to remove them, we will, to the extent that medium allows it.

You can delete your comments whenever you want. We may remove any content that doesn't follow the Community Rules or these terms.

## 6. Organizers

- Anyone with an account can become an organizer.
- **Their plans are published without prior review**, and the app's team reviews them afterwards. It can unpublish a plan or remove an account's organizer role if it breaks these rules.
- Posting has a **free monthly quota**, which the app shows when you create each plan.

## 7. What happens if these rules are broken

Depending on how serious and repeated it is, we may:
- remove content;
- block the account from commenting;
- remove the organizer role.

## 8. Changes to these terms

If we change something relevant, we'll let you know in the app. To keep saving, commenting or posting you'll need to accept the new version. Each version states at the top since when it is in force.
