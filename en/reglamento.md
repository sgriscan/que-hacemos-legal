*This is a translation of the [Spanish version]({{ '/es/reglamento/' | relative_url }}). If they differ in any way, the Spanish version prevails.*

Comments are here for one thing only: **so that what you already know helps the people who go next.** When to arrive, what to bring, what to avoid, whether there's somewhere to sit, whether the café next door is worth it.

## What is not allowed

- Insults, aggression or threats, towards anyone.
- Discrimination for any reason.
- Posting other people's personal data: names, phone numbers, photos, addresses.
- Advertising, spam or promotional links.
- Illegal content.
- Impersonating someone else, including using their name as your display name.
- Commenting on a venue or organizer you have a conflict of interest with: your own, a relative's, a competitor's.

The same rules apply, where relevant, to what organizers post.

## What happens if someone breaks them

- **The comment is removed.** It stops being visible to everyone.
- **If it happens again,** the account is **blocked from commenting.** It can still use the rest of the app.

## How to report

If you see a comment that breaks these rules, tap **Report** below it. A person from the team reviews it. Meanwhile the comment stays visible. Reporting doesn't notify the person who wrote it.
