This policy explains which personal data **¿Qué hacemos?** (the "app") uses, what for, who it is shared with, and how you can exercise your rights.

## 2. Which data we use and what for

We only use the data the app needs to work, and nothing else.

| Data | What for | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| An identifier for a session without an account | Letting you use the app without signing up: browsing plans and opening their details. It doesn't identify you as a person. | Yes, to use the app. |
| Email | Identifying your account, signing in, sending registration and password recovery codes. | Yes, to have an account. |
| Password | Signing in. Stored in a way nobody can read it, us included. | Yes, to have an account. |
| Display name | Signing your comments. | Yes, to have an account. |
| Profile photo and description | Showing your profile. | No. |
| Saved plans | Your Saved list and your Agenda. | No. |
| Comments you write | Showing them on the plan or organizer you commented on, and using them to promote the app, **without your name or photo** (see the [Terms]({{ '/en/terminos/' | relative_url }}), section 5). | No. |
| Reports you make about comments | Letting an administrator review them. | No. |
| Which version of the terms you accepted, and when | Recording your consent. | Yes, to have an account. |
| The time of certain actions (posting, commenting, reporting) | Preventing abuse by limiting how often something can be done. Deleted automatically after 24 hours. | Yes, while you use those features. |
| If you are an organizer: name, type (person or organization), photo, description and the plans you post | Showing your organizer profile and your plans, and using your plans to promote the app. | Yes, to be an organizer. |

**What we don't use:** we don't access your location. When an organizer enters a venue's address, the phone's own service turns it into a point on the map; nobody's location is used. We don't sell your data or use it to show you ads. Your display name and profile photo never appear in the app's promotion unless you authorize it separately.

If you don't provide a required piece of data, you can't use the part of the app that needs it. For example, you can't create an account without an email. The rest of the app keeps working.

## 3. What is public

**Anyone can see, even without an account:**

- the text of your comments;
- your display name and profile photo, next to each comment you write;
- if you are an organizer, your organizer profile and the plans you post.

Your email is never shown to other people.

## 4. Who it is shared with

To work, the app uses providers that process data on our behalf, only to provide their service:

- **Supabase:** hosts the database, the accounts and the images.
- **Resend:** sends the emails with codes.
- **GitHub Pages:** publishes these legal texts. It doesn't receive any account data.

We don't share your data with anyone else, unless a competent authority requires it under the law.

## 5. How long we keep your data

- **While you have an account,** we keep your account data.
- **If you delete it,** your email, password, name, photo and description, saved plans, comments, reports and terms acceptance records are removed at that moment.
  - If you were an organizer, your organizer profile and your plans are removed too, together with their comments.
  - Images are deleted right after. If that step fails, they remain as files with no data linking them to you, and we delete them when we detect it.
- **The time of certain actions** is deleted automatically after 24 hours.
- **The session without an account** stays on the phone while the app is installed. <span class="pendiente">[COMPLETAR: plazo de borrado de las sesiones sin cuenta sin uso.]</span>

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data: know which data of yours we have. Ask through the contact in section 1.
- **Rectify** it: correct whatever is wrong. You can change your name, photo and description yourself from **Account**.
- **Delete** it: you can do it yourself at any time from **Account → Delete my account**, without asking anyone.

Access requests are free of charge. <span class="pendiente">[A VERIFICAR: plazos de respuesta de la Ley 25.326 (arts. 14 y 16).]</span>

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we change something relevant, we'll let you know in the app. To keep saving, commenting or posting you'll need to accept the new version. Each version states at the top since when it is in force.
